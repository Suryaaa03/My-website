Capcom Equity Research: Nanyang Capital Stock Pitch

A BUY call on Capcom (TSE: 9697) with a ¥4,800 base-case target, built on a full three-statement and DCF model.

My role
Built the financial model and investment thesis
When
2026
Report & files ↓

Overview

Capcom's share price had fallen about 27% from its peak after the troubled launch of Monster Hunter Wilds, as investors worried that FY2025 had been the top of the cycle. For the Nanyang Capital Stock Pitch 2026 I argued the market was wrong: catalog sales were accelerating, guidance pointed to a 13th straight year of record profit, and upcoming launches weren't priced in.

I built the valuation from Capcom's annual and integrated reports: segment-level revenue projections, three financial statements, a DCF, peer comparison and probability-weighted scenarios.

What I did

  1. Compiled five years of financials (FY2021–FY2025) and projected revenue by segment (Digital Contents, Arcade Operations, Amusement Equipment, Other) to FY2028, driven by unit sales of major titles and catalog growth.
  2. Built the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow projections, then valued the company by DCF (WACC 8.7%, terminal growth 2.5%) weighted with comparable-company multiples and scenario analysis.
  3. Benchmarked Capcom against gaming peers on EV/EBITDA, P/E, operating margin and revenue growth to test whether its valuation premium was justified.
  4. Wrote a variant view on five market concerns and mapped catalysts (Resident Evil Requiem launch, Monster Hunter Wilds DLC, Switch 2 ports) against consensus estimates.
  5. Set bear, base and bull cases with explicit revenue, margin, EPS and exit-multiple assumptions, plus a structured risk matrix with mitigants.

Results

  • Recommendation: BUY, 12-month base-case target ¥4,800 (+30.8% from ¥3,670)
  • Probability-weighted target of ¥4,550 across bear (¥2,800), base (¥4,800) and bull (¥5,400) cases
