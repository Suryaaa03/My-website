ARROW: Maritime Intelligence Platform

A shipbroking platform that pairs an AI market briefing (RAG) with route optimisation and fuel-strategy analysis.

Overview

Shipbrokers negotiate fixtures between shipowners and charterers using a mix of regulations, market reports and route knowledge scattered across dozens of documents. For the ARROW case competition our team built a web platform that brings this together: define a cargo and route, plan the voyage, compare fuel strategies, and get an AI-written market briefing grounded in real regulatory and market documents.

My part was the RAG pipeline behind the briefings and the AI assistant, which answers questions with maritime information drawn only from the document library.

What I did

  1. Indexed a library of 15 maritime documents (SOLAS, MARPOL, the ISM and ISPS codes, BIMCO charter parties, ISO 8217 fuel standards and Veson market reports) into a Chroma vector store using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence embeddings.
  2. Retrieved the most relevant passages for each voyage's inputs (ports, cargo, fuel type, laycan dates) and listed the source documents alongside each answer.
  3. Prompted Mistral-7B-Instruct (via Hugging Face) to write a structured 800–900-word broker briefing covering route risks, cargo market conditions, fuel and compliance costs, negotiation leverage and valuation ranges, using only the retrieved context.
  4. Served the pipeline as a Flask API, packaged with Docker, that feeds the platform's dashboard and chat assistant.
  5. The team's route optimiser generated 3,789 candidate sea routes between export and import ports (direct and via hub ports), flagged high-risk war zones and ranked routes by distance, port charges and expected demurrage.
  6. The fuel-strategy module compares a baseline fuel mix against alternative fuels and energy-saving devices over the vessel's life, with carbon pooling prices, discounting, ROI and price-sensitivity analysis.

Results

  • End-to-end prototype: web dashboard, RAG briefing API and route optimiser working together
  • Briefings cite the regulatory and market documents they draw from, so brokers can check every claim
  • 3,789 pre-computed routes, mapped interactively with Folium
