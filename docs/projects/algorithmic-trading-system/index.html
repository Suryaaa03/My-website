What I did

  1. Pull live and historical equity data through broker and market-data APIs.
  2. Generate rule-based signals from indicators such as moving averages and RSI.
  3. Design parameter-sensitivity and out-of-sample tests before backtesting, to avoid overfitting to past data.

Results & conclusions

Conclusion

Still in progress. Results will be added once out-of-sample testing is complete.

What I learnt

Even before backtesting, I've learnt why so many strategies fail on new data: it's easy to tune parameters until the past looks great. Designing the sensitivity and out-of-sample tests first keeps me honest about whether a signal is real.
