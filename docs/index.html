About

I'm a Data Science and Economics undergraduate at the National University of Singapore. I like taking messy, real-world data and turning it into something people can make decisions with, whether that's a forecast, a valuation or a policy recommendation.

Before university I served as a Combat Engineer Officer in the Singapore Armed Forces, where I led explosives training for 100 soldiers and took my unit through two major evaluations. It taught me to plan carefully, stay calm under pressure and own the outcome.

Since then I've built a Python emissions-compliance optimiser for a shipping company's fleet, forecast humidity with LSTM and XGBoost models, built a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline for maritime regulations, and valued companies with DCF models for stock pitch competitions. I also tutor mathematics at Tim Gan Math. I'm happiest where data science meets economics and finance.
